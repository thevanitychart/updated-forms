Hi, I'm Chi 🩷. I'm a DNP candidate and practicing RN, based in the Miami, Florida metropolitan area. I've always had a love for makeup and skincare, a self-taught makeup lover for over a decade. Now I bring a clinical eye to beauty, helping people navigate an overwhelming industry with real product knowledge instead of guesswork or sales pressure.

Welcome to The Vanity Chart, your personal makeup and skincare chart.

I offer a few ways to work together, from full sessions to quick specialty add-ons, pick what fits your needs:

Virtual Consult, a video or text-based session where we talk through your skin, goals, and current routine, and I send you a personalized product list afterward. Best if you want a plan without leaving home.

In-Person Shopping/Consult, where I meet you at Sephora, Ulta, or your preferred retailer to test products together in real time. Buying is optional, this is about finding what actually works for you, not a pressure to purchase.

Declutter & Edit, where we sort through what's already in your vanity, deciding what to keep, toss, or replace. Best if you already own a lot of products and just need an honest edit.

Across every session: no pressure to buy anything you don't love, no judgment on your current routine, just honest advice from someone with a clinical background who genuinely loves this stuff.

No wrong answers here. This isn't a test or a critique of your current routine, it's just so I can get to know your skin, your style, and what you're hoping for. Come as you are.

What to expect: We'll chat, I'll ask a few questions, and there's zero pressure to buy anything you don't love. This is your session.
