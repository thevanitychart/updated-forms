Client information

What this covers

The Vanity Chart provides personalized beauty consulting, shopping guidance, and product recommendations. I am not a licensed esthetician, dermatologist, or cosmetologist, and I do not apply makeup, skincare products, or perform any hands-on treatments during our session. My recommendations are based on my clinical nursing background and personal expertise, but they are not medical advice.

Patch testing & allergies

Everyone's skin reacts differently to products, even ones marketed as gentle or hypoallergenic. I strongly recommend patch-testing any new product on a small area of skin (like your inner wrist) 24-48 hours before full use, especially if you have known sensitivities, allergies, or reactive skin.

I will do my best to avoid recommending ingredients you've flagged as concerns, but I cannot guarantee a product won't cause a reaction, and I am not liable for allergic reactions, irritation, or other adverse effects from products I recommend or that you purchase during our session.

Photos & privacy

Any photos you share with me before or during our session are kept private and used only to prepare for and personalize your consultation. They will not be shared, posted, or used for marketing unless you separately opt in on your intake form.
